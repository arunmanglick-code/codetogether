Observability projects built around the Grafana ecosystem, including dashboard creation for visualizing metrics, Telegraf-based data collection from infrastructure and applications, and InfluxDB for time-series data storage. Covers building end-to-end monitoring stacks that provide real-time insight into system performance. These projects demonstrate open-source observability tooling as an alternative to commercial platforms.
